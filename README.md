## ✨ Key Automation Benefits

* **24/7 Availability:** Website and WhatsApp bots respond to customers anytime, even late at night.
* **Instant Response:** AI chatbot instantly answers queries and builds customer trust.
* **Zero Data Loss:** Every booking is automatically saved in Google Sheets.
* **Auto Reminders:** Customers receive automatic reminders for upcoming appointments.
* **Weekly Insights:** A complete weekly performance report is sent via email.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Integration

* **Workflow Engine:** n8n (Self-hosted)
* **AI Model:** GPT-based chatbot
* **Database:** Google Sheets (Booking Logs)
* **Communication:** WhatsApp API & Gmail (Notifications)

---

## 💡 Overview

This system is designed for service-based businesses (plumbing, electrical, etc.), where:

* Customers can book services via website, chatbot, or WhatsApp
* The system automatically stores all booking data
* Email notifications and reminders are sent automatically
* Business owners receive weekly performance reports

👉 In simple terms: **Your business runs on autopilot.**

## 💰 Estimated Monthly Running Cost

The system has a low operational cost depending on usage:

* **Server Hosting (n8n):** $5 – $10/month
* **WhatsApp API:** $2 – $20/month (based on conversations)
* **AI Chatbot:** $1 – $5/month

👉 **Total Estimated Cost:**
Approximately **$10 – $30 per month**

> Note: Costs may vary depending on the number of users and messages.
